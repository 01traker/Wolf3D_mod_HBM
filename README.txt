This is a README file
A mod for Wolfenstein 3D, made for ECWolf v1.4.2.

Includes 13 levels with new enemies, weapons, decorations, items, treasure,
and textures.
[Installation]
#[Windows]
Drag and drop the mod file onto the ECWolf executable. If prompted, select: ```Wolfenstein 3D (*.WL6)```



Finished in the moth 9 of the year 2026

Feel free to use and modify, share, take or replace any part of this mod to your liking. This is a mod after all not a paid product (and even then it would be PWYW model).

Some music is from youtube or brutal wolfenstein mod for DooM
All levels can be completed on "Death Incarnate" with pistol start
Saving is permitted
Not tested on lower difficulties


What is new in this mod?
There are 13 levels with varying quality and difficulty.
Many maps were made as I went on with the project, thus they can appear underbaked
[Base changes]
Ammo is capped at 300
Dog food heals up to 175
Food heals up to 150
Red medikits heal up to 125
Une up health gives all ammo types and overheals to 200 health
Blood & gibs overheals to 21 health

[Background]
This mod would've released about a year ago (almost to the date) 
but I lost interest and got sidetracked with other stuff, so I am finishing it now 
(after somebody kinda pushed me into it from totally different fandom).

How this mod came to be?
As a child I played a lot of old video games, one of them was "Beyond Wolfenstein 2 Special edition",
despite not being finished and some things were broken, I still had a good time.
Later when I rediscovered wolf 3D I was surprised how "lamer" it was compared to
that one. So I searched and found some remains and other stuff.
All this then lead to me creating this mod.
Is it balanced? No.
Is it fun? No.
Does it scratch that itch that I have? Perhaps. And that is probably why it exists.
